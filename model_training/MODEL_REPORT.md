## 6. Generalisation Analysis

### 6.1 Within-Dataset Generalisation

UCSD provides a pre-defined train/test split. The test set was never seen during training.

> **Note:** Both Peds1 and Peds2 show the same university walkway location.
> This is **intra-venue generalisation only** — not cross-venue generalisation.

### 6.2 Cross-Venue Generalisation

**NOT TESTED.** Only one venue type (UCSD walkway) is available.

To claim cross-venue generalisation for CrowdGuard AI, the models must be evaluated on:
- Concert hall recordings
- Auditorium entry/exit sequences
- Convention hall footage
- Indoor stadium recordings

### 6.3 Known Generalisation Risks

| Risk Factor | Status |
|---|---|
| Different lighting | Not evaluated (UCSD = outdoor, daylight only) |
| Indoor environments | Not evaluated |
| High crowd density (concert) | Not in UCSD dataset |
| Crowd surges / stampede precursors | Not in UCSD dataset |
| Different camera angles | Peds1 vs Peds2 provides some variation |
| Night footage | Not available |
| Partial occlusion | Partially present in Peds1 |

## 9. Limitations and Deployment Cautions

### 9.1 Technical Limitations

1. **CPU-only training.** No GPU available. Models are kept small to compensate.
2. **Simplified feature extraction.** Optical flow uses gradient approximation, not full Farneback algorithm.
3. **Small dataset.** ~19,600 frames total across both subsets.
4. **Binary labels only.** Cannot train 4-class risk model.

### 9.2 Safety Limitations

> ⚠️ **These models must NOT be used for operational crowd safety decisions without:**

1. Independent validation on actual venue recordings
2. Expert review by certified crowd-safety professionals
3. Sensitivity/specificity analysis against real events
4. Integration with physical alarm and evacuation protocols
5. Human-in-the-loop review of all high-risk predictions

### 9.3 Ethical Considerations

- Surveillance of individuals without consent raises privacy concerns
- False positives may cause unnecessary alarm
- False negatives may provide false confidence
- The model has no ability to predict physical crowd crush forces
- Algorithmic bias: trained on US university walkway demographics
